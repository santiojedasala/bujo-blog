## Introduction
Starting a bullet journal can feel overwhelming when you see all the beautiful spreads online. But the truth is: you don't need a lot of supplies to begin. In fact, keeping it simple is the best way to stick with it. This guide covers the only supplies you really need as a beginner.

## 1. A Good Notebook
The most important supply is the notebook itself. Look for one with dotted grid pages and thick paper (at least 100 GSM) so your pens don't bleed through. Popular beginner-friendly options include the Scribbles That Matter notebook and the Leuchtturm1917 A5.

## 2. A Black Fineliner Pen
A reliable fineliner is your daily workhorse. The Sakura Pigma Micron set (sizes 01, 03, 05) is affordable, archival quality, and doesn't smudge. You can use it for outlines, writing, and basic doodles.

## 3. A Ruler or Straight Edge
Simple but essential. A small 15 cm ruler fits inside most notebook pockets and helps you draw clean lines for spreads, trackers, and grids.

## 4. Washi Tape (Optional but Fun)
Washi tape adds color and personality to your pages without much effort. You can use it to create borders, mark sections, or simply decorate. A basic starter set with 10-15 rolls costs very little.

## 5. A Pencil and Eraser
Always sketch your layouts in pencil first before going over them with pen. A simple mechanical pencil and a white eraser work perfectly.

## 6. A Basic Stencil Set
If you struggle with drawing neat shapes or banners, a bullet journal stencil set is a cheap solution. It helps you create consistent layouts quickly.

## 7. A Pencil Case or Pouch
Keep everything organized in one place. A simple zipper pouch or pen case makes it easy to take your journal supplies wherever you go.

## Conclusion
You don't need expensive supplies to start a bullet journal. With a notebook, a good pen, and a few extras, you can create a system that helps you stay organized and creative. Start simple, and add supplies only when you need them.
